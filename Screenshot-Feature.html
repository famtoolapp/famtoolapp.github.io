On-Demand Screenshot

Instantly capture what's on your child's screen, no matter what app they are using, for a quick safety check.

Feature Status: Active & Released

Now Available for Everyone!

See exactly what your child sees in real-time. The On-Demand Screenshot feature is now fully released and available for all users. You can capture a high-quality image of the target device's current screen with just a single click from your dashboard. This powerful tool is perfect for understanding the context of conversations, viewing social media activity, or ensuring safety across any app.

Compatibility

Fully optimized for Android 10 to Android 15. No root access required. Simply click to capture and get instant visibility.
