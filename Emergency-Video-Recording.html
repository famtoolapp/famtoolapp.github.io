Emergency Video Recording

In critical situations, see and hear what's happening around your child. Remotely record video from their phone's camera for complete peace of mind.

See Their World in Motion

A single picture provides a snapshot, but a video offers the full story. With FamToolApp's Emergency Video Recording, you can remotely activate the camera to record a short video clip. This powerful tool helps you assess your child's environment, understand the context of their situation, and ensure their safety when you can't be physically present.

Having Trouble Recording?

Newer Android versions have enhanced security that might block camera access when the app is in the background. If a command doesn't work, this is likely the cause. You can easily fix this by re-enabling the app's Accessibility Service. Please follow the simple steps outlined in our Installation Guide to resolve this.
