File Review for Safety

Review the files and folders stored on your child's device to help them manage their data and stay safe from harmful downloads.

Feature Status: Beta Testing

Coming Soon to All Users!

Are you curious about the files your child is downloading or creating? Our upcoming File Review feature will give you a secure window into the device's storage. You'll be able to browse folders and view file details to ensure your child isn't exposed to inappropriate material and to help them back up important memories.

An Exclusive First Look

This feature is currently in the final stages of testing and is in beta version. We have given access to a select group of users to help us refine and perfect its functionality.

Once the testing is complete, the File Review feature will be automatically available in your parent panel. We are working hard to make it available to everyone very soon. Thank you for your patience!
