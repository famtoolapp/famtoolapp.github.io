Voice & Ambient Recording

Listen to the surroundings of your child's phone to ensure they are in a safe environment and understand the context of their situation.

Hear What Matters for Their Safety

Sometimes, visual information isn't enough. FamToolApp's Voice Recording feature lets you discreetly record the ambient sound around your child's device. Hear their conversations and surroundings to make sure they are not in danger or uncomfortable situations.

Having Trouble Recording?

Newer Android versions have enhanced security that might block microphone access when the app is in the background. If a command doesn't work, this is likely the cause. You can easily fix this by re-enabling the app's Accessibility Service. Please follow the simple steps outlined in our Installation Guide to resolve this.
